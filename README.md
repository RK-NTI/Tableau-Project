# 📊 Amazon Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard (Tableau-Project)

• Designed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze Amazon Sales Performance.

• Conducted in depth research on actionable insights to support sales performance monitoring, customer analysis, product performance evaluation & making business responsible business decisions.


# 📌 Key KPIs: 

💰 Total Sales: 642.13M

🛒 Total Orders: 10,000

👥 Total Customers: 6,016

📦 Total Quantity: 30,144

🏷️ Average Discount: 15%

# 🔍 What I analyzed: 

• Top 10 Customers by Sales

• Category-wise Sales Performance

• Sub-Category Sales using a Treemap

• Top 4 Products by Sales

• Payment Method Analysis

• State-wise Sales Performance

# 🔑 Key Insights

🔹Total Sales reached 642.13M, indicating strong overall sales performance across the business.

🔹Home is the highest-performing category with approximately 214.76M in sales, followed by Electronics (219.36M) and Fashion (208.01M).

🔹Furniture leads the sub-categories with around 111.84M in sales, making it the strongest sub-category in the dashboard.

🔹 Footwear and Clothing also contribute significantly, with approximately 104.01M and 104.00M respectively.
 
🔹COD is the most-used payment method at approximately 25.5%, closely followed by Card (25.1%), while UPI and Net Banking each account for around 24.7%.

🔹The state-wise map shows noticeable variation in sales across different states, helping identify stronger and weaker geographical markets.

🔹The overall average discount is 15%, providing a useful metric for evaluating the relationship between discounting and sales performance.
